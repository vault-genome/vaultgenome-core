# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

This project builds infrastructure intended to preserve continuity of AI
systems and the data centers behind them through catastrophic loss, including
during active war. The people working on it deserve a space that is technically
rigorous, respectful, and resilient to stress. In turn, contributors are
expected to extend the same to each other.

We follow the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1**, with the project-specific
additions below. The full text is available at:

    https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/

The full Contributor Covenant text will be inlined into this file before the
first public release. Until then, the reference above is binding in conjunction
with the section below.

## Project-Specific Standards

- **Technical honesty.** Pushback on architectural decisions is welcome.
  Fabrication of data, test results, or attestations is a terminating offense.
- **Attribution discipline.** When you cite a patent, a design document, or a
  prior contribution, cite it accurately. Silent appropriation of doctrinal
  decisions made by others is a kind of dishonesty that damages trust.
- **Calm under pressure.** The system is designed to operate under war
  conditions. The community should be able to, too. Escalating personal
  conflict during an operational incident is explicitly unacceptable.
- **Security-first disclosure.** If you discover a vulnerability during your
  contribution work, report it privately per `SECURITY.md`. Do not post it in
  an issue, a PR, or a chat.

## Enforcement

Reports may be sent to the maintainers named in [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md),
at `nikolaichuk.s.f@gmail.com`. All reports are kept confidential
to the fullest extent compatible with investigating and resolving the matter.

Enforcement actions follow the Contributor Covenant enforcement ladder:
correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban. Fabrication of security
evidence or deliberate weakening of a doctrinal invariant is an automatic
permanent ban.
